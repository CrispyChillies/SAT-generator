Subject: SAT | Section: Reading and Writing | Category: Information and Ideas | Difficulty: Easy | Type: multiple-choice
In recent studies of urban transportation, researchers analyzed the impact of electric vehicles (EVs) on reducing carbon emissions in major cities. Their findings indicated that in the Northeast region, when more EVs were deployed than initially forecasted, the actual reduction in carbon emissions surpassed predictions. For instance, data showed that the reduction in emissions was approximately 120,000 metric tons, compared to the original estimate. This indicates that when the number of EVs on the road is higher than anticipated, the environmental benefits can be significantly greater than expected. For example, researchers found that in the Northeast, the actual reduction was ______
Which choice most effectively uses data from the study to complete the example?

Choices:

A. A. approximately 120 thousand metric tons less than expected.
B. B. approximately 120 thousand metric tons more than expected.
C. C. significantly less than the projected reduction of 125 thousand metric tons.
D. D. only slightly above the expected reduction of 115 thousand metric tons.

Explanation:

Choice B is the correct answer. It accurately completes the sentence by stating that the actual reduction in carbon emissions was approximately 120 thousand metric tons more than expected, consistent with the data provided in the paragraph. Choice A is incorrect because it states that the reduction was less than expected, which contradicts the findings that indicated a greater reduction than predicted. Choice C is flawed as it incorrectly suggests that the actual reduction was significantly less than projected, misrepresenting the data that shows a better outcome. Choice D is irrelevant because it implies that the reduction was only slightly above the expected amount, which contradicts the strong evidence of a much larger reduction presented in the paragraph.
