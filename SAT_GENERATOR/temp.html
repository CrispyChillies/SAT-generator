Subject: SAT | Section: Reading and Writing | Category: Information and Ideas | Difficulty: Easy | Type: multiple-choice
A recent study was conducted to analyze consumer preferences in a retail environment. Researchers created a fictional online store that offered five different brands of headphones. They divided participants into two groups: one that viewed the headphones with a ranking system titled "Top Rated Headphones," and another that viewed the same products without any rankings. The researchers measured the time participants spent reading product descriptions. Their goal was to determine how rankings influence consumer attention and decision-making when selecting headphones.

Which choice best describes data in the table that support the researchers' conclusion?

Choices:

A. A. Participants who were not provided with a ranking of the headphones spent the most time reading about the TuneMaster 3000.
B. B. Participants who were provided with a ranking of the headphones spent significantly more time reading about SoundWave Pro.
C. C. Participants who were provided with a ranking of the headphones spent the least time reading about BassBoost X.
D. D. Participants who were not provided with a ranking of the headphones spent roughly equal amounts of time reading about each brand.

Explanation:

Choice B is the best answer because the data show that participants who viewed the ranked list spent 40% of their time reading about the SoundWave Pro, which is significantly more than the other brands. This supports the conclusion that rankings influence consumer attention. The other choices do not accurately reflect the data; Choice A misstates the time spent on TuneMaster 3000, Choice C incorrectly describes the time spent on BassBoost X, and Choice D incorrectly states that time spent was equal among brands.
